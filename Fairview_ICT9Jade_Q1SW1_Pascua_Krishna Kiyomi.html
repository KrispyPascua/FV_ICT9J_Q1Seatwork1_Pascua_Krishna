My Top 3 Spotify Artists

  1. John Michael Howell
    I like this artist because of his emotionally powerful songwriting. Songs such as “Story of a Warrior” and “Missing Piece” make listeners like me feel as though we are experiencing the soundtrack of a movie—each song telling a story and evoking vivid emotions.
  2. Alex Warren
    I really enjoy listening to Alex Warren because songs such as “Ordinary” and “Burning Down” showcase a deep sense of vulnerability. Much of his appeal comes from the difficult experiences he has faced, which give his music an authentic and emotional quality.
  3. Adele
    Her songs really resonate with me because of her instantly recognizable voice. It is powerful, warm, and slightly raspy, turning songs like “Rolling in the Deep” and “Set Fire to the Rain” into emotional climaxes that leave a lasting impression.
